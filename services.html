Overview

A single studio, start to finish.

Most luxury brands juggle a designer, a materials agent, a factory and a freight forwarder, and lose something in every hand-off. All Pack brings the whole chain together, so the box you approved in the prototype is the box that arrives on your shelves.

We work with fragrance houses, distilleries, jewellers, couture labels and fine-food makers. Our clients tend to share one trait: they would rather wait for the right answer than settle for a quick one. Projects begin with a conversation, move through physical prototypes you can hold, and end with production that is inspected piece by piece.

01 · Custom packaging design

Structures designed around your product.

We begin with the object, its weight, its proportions, the way it should be revealed, and engineer the packaging outward from there.

  • Structural engineering. Magnetic fold-backs, shoulder-neck boxes, drawer sleeves, book-style cases, clamshells and bespoke formats with precision-cut inserts.
  • Brand and graphic direction. Typography, monograms and surface artwork prepared for foil, emboss and print, with every layer specified.
  • Unboxing sequence. We choreograph the reveal: what the customer sees first, what they hear, and what they feel as the lid lifts.
  • Physical prototyping. White samples and fully finished pre-production prototypes, so decisions are made with the real object in your hands.

02 · Material selection & sourcing

The right material says it first.

We hold relationships with specialist mills and finishers across Europe and Asia, and we trace every board, wrap and ribbon back to its source.

Rigid greyboard

Dense 2–3 mm board gives a rigid box its architectural, weighty feel. FSC-certified as standard.

Linen & cloth wraps

Woven textures in natural, dyed and metallic threads, with a hand that reads as craft rather than print.

Fine papers

Cotton-rag, textured and soft-touch stocks from heritage mills, including uncoated papers that take a deep blind emboss.

Velvet & suede linings

Flocked and brushed interiors in deep jewel tones to cradle watches, jewellery and objects of value.

Metal & hardware

Brushed-brass hinges, magnetic closures, engraved plates and ribbon in satin, grosgrain or silk.

Responsible alternatives

Recycled boards, plant-based leathers, water-based coatings and plastic-free inserts, specified without compromise to the finish.

03 · Production & finishing

Made with patience, inspected with obsession.

Our production partners are audited by us, not just vetted on paper. Every run follows the same disciplined path, and nothing ships until it has passed a final hand inspection.

  1. Step one

    Brief & dieline

    We confirm dimensions, quantities, budget and brand intent, then draw the structure to the millimetre.

  2. Step two

    Prototype & proof

    A finished sample with real materials and real foils, refined across up to three rounds until it is right.

  3. Step three

    Production

    Cutting, wrapping, finishing and assembly, with in-line quality checks at every stage.

  4. Step four

    Inspection & delivery

    Piece-by-piece review, protective packing and delivery flat-packed or fully assembled, on your schedule.

Finishes we perfect

  • Hot-foil stamping in gold, copper, silver and pigment foils
  • Blind, debossed and multi-level embossing
  • Spot UV and soft-touch lamination
  • Edge painting and gilding
  • Silk-screen and tactile ink effects
  • Custom-fit foam, card and moulded-pulp inserts
