About All Pack

A studio built on patience.

For fifteen years we have made packaging for brands that refuse to rush the details: small teams, rare materials and a finish checked by eye, one unit at a time.

Our story

It began with a single box.

In 2010, our founder Eleanor Marchetti was commissioned to pack a small run of hand-poured perfumes. The brief was simple. The boxes available were not.

Stock cartons felt hollow. Overseas suppliers promised luxury and delivered warped lids and smudged foil. So Eleanor rented a bench in a Clerkenwell workshop, learned rigid-box construction from a retired bookbinder, and made the first two hundred boxes herself.

The perfumer's customers wrote in about the packaging before they wrote about the scent. That told us what All Pack should be: a studio where structure, material and finish are treated as one discipline, and where no brand is too small to deserve extraordinary work.

Core values

What we hold to account.

Four principles shape every proposal, prototype and production run. They are the reason clients stay with us for years rather than projects.

01

Restraint

We edit relentlessly. One perfectly executed finish carries more authority than five competing for attention.

02

Precision

Tolerances of under half a millimetre, corners that meet, foil that registers exactly. Luxury is found in what never goes wrong.

03

Candour

We tell you when a finish will not survive transit or a budget will not stretch, and we offer an alternative that will.

04

Responsibility

FSC-certified boards, plastic-free laminates and reusable structures wherever the brief allows, because beautiful should also be responsible.

Craftsmanship

Finished by hand, judged by eye.

Machines give us consistency. People give us standards. Every All Pack commission passes through both.

  • Physical prototypes first. You hold, open and test a white sample, then a fully finished one, before any production is approved.
  • Colour and foil proofing. Pantone-matched drawdowns and foil swatches are signed off under daylight-balanced light.
  • Three-point inspection. Checks at board cutting, wrapping and final assembly, with every unit in a luxury run inspected individually.
  • Packed for the journey. Transit-tested cartons and protective inners so the box arrives as it left us.
