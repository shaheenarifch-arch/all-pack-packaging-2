What happens next

A reply within one working day.

Every enquiry is read by a designer, not a call centre. You will hear from a named person who has understood your brief, with initial thoughts and the next sensible step.

  1. Within 1 working day

    Personal reply

    We acknowledge your brief, answer early questions and suggest a time to talk.

  2. Within 3 days

    Discovery conversation

    A call or studio visit to explore materials, finishes, quantities and budget.

  3. Within 10 days

    Proposal and samples

    A clear written proposal with structural direction, finish swatches and pricing.

  4. 2 to 3 weeks

    First prototype

    A hand-built white sample in your hands to test fit, feel and the opening sequence.
